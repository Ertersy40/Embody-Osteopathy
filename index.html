ABOUT

Our Story

Embody Healthcare Osteopathy was established in Greensborough in 2002 by osteopath Kathryn Williams and has been assisting the community for over 20 years. We have recently moved to an inviting new premises in Bolton Street, Eltham. At Embody Healthcare we have five experienced osteopaths offering treatment for the whole family, from newborns to the elderly.
